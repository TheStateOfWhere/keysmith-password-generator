Security & privacy

Readable uses three distinct words with consistent case and fixed periods. Never includes l L i I o O 0 1; suffix symbols are - _ @ # % * +. Random profiles follow configured categories, minimum counts and exclusions. All profiles enforce at least 64 bits of calculated entropy. Matching configured rules does not guarantee compliance with an unspecified customer policy or resistance to offline attacks. Secure randomness and validation run in your browser.

Passwords stay in page memory. Clearing or reloading removes them. Only your selected theme identifier is saved in this browser’s localStorage, when available. Passwords, policies, clipboard contents and random state are never saved. No password storage, telemetry, or password network requests. Copying is explicit; your operating system may retain clipboard history.
